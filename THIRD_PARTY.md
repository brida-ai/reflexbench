# Third-party provenance

ReflexBench code/docs and Brida-authored synthetic fixtures are released under Apache-2.0 unless a file states otherwise.

Third-party models and datasets retain their own terms. ReflexBench does not relicense them.

- **TypeSafe Jev** is a hosted proprietary System One model; ReflexBench publishes Brida measurement receipts, not Jev weights.
- **JevBench** is an external benchmark. Raw external held-out items are not redistributed here unless their upstream license explicitly permits it; ReflexBench publishes hashes/provenance and Brida result receipts.
- **Amazon MASSIVE**, **Banking77**, **CLINC150**, **HINT3**, **MTOP** and other external datasets/models referenced by research reports must be obtained from their canonical sources under their own licenses.
- **Laya, Kev, jeff, Verdict, Qwen/Reflex and other open models** retain their upstream model/code licenses. Exact revisions are recorded in receipts/engine registry when available.

Before redistribution or commercial use, review the upstream license for every model/dataset you use.
